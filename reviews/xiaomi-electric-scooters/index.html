How to choose the right Xiaomi scooter in the UAE

1. Size range to your real route, not the marketing number

3–5 km a day? A 20–25 km scooter is fine. 15–25 km a day? Get at least 45–60 km claimed. For long commutes, look at the 6 Max, 4 Ultra or 5 Plus.

2. Don’t ignore tyre size

On UAE roads, 10-inch beats 8.5-inch, and 12-inch is more comfortable and stable again. If comfort matters, look at the Scooter 6, 5 Plus or 6 Max.

3. Suspension matters if you ride daily

Occasional riding is fine without it. Daily riders feel the difference in wrist comfort and control. The 6 Lite adds front suspension; the 6, 4 Ultra, 5 Plus and 6 Max go further.

4. Heavier riders should skip the weakest models

Carrying weight or a backpack? A stronger motor, bigger battery, larger tyres and better brakes pay off every day. The 6, 5 Plus, 4 Ultra and 6 Max carry 120–130 kg.

5. Check the UAE rules before you ride

Every Xiaomi here is capped at 25 km/h, matching the UAE limit — but you still need a free RTA permit (see the official RTA e-scooter page), a helmet, and you must stay in designated zones. Riding outside them risks AED 200–500 fines. See the full Dubai e-scooter law guide.

Xiaomi electric scooter UAE — FAQs

Which Xiaomi electric scooter is best in the UAE 2026?

For most riders the Scooter 6 (AED 1,668) is the best value — 45 km range, 12-inch tubeless tyres, full suspension and an 800W peak motor. For the longest range, the 6 Max (AED 2,695) leads with 70 km and 1100W peak.

What is the cheapest Xiaomi scooter in the UAE?

The Essential (2025) at AED 699 on Noon, followed by the 4 Lite at AED 899 on Amazon. Both suit short, flat trips rather than long commutes.

Which Xiaomi scooter has the longest range?

The 6 Max and 4 Ultra both claim 70 km. The 6 Max holds 70 km even at full 25 km/h; the 4 Ultra reaches it in eco conditions. Expect 20–30% less in Dubai heat.

Are Xiaomi electric scooters legal in Dubai?

They’re capped at 25 km/h, matching the UAE limit. To ride legally you need a free RTA permit, a helmet, and you must stay in designated zones. Rules vary by emirate — see our Dubai law guide.

Which Xiaomi is best under AED 1,200?

The 6 Lite at AED 1,199 — it adds front dual-spring suspension over the 4 Lite, with 10-inch tyres, a 500W peak motor and 25 km range.

Is the 4 Lite 2nd Gen worth it?

Yes for budget buyers. At AED 1,059 it moves to 10-inch pneumatic tyres and 25 km range with drum + E-ABS brakes — a real upgrade over the original 4 Lite.

Amazon or Noon for a Xiaomi scooter?

Whichever has the lower checkout price, higher seller rating and clearer warranty. Amazon lists more models; Noon has exclusives like the Essential, Elite and 5 Max.

How much real range do Xiaomi scooters get in Dubai heat?

Plan for 20–30% below the claim. A 25 km scooter realistically does 17–20 km; a 70 km model lands around 50–55 km — less with a heavier rider, headwind or full-speed riding in 40°C+ heat.

What’s the difference between the 5, 5 Plus and 5 Max?

The base 5 has front suspension and 60 km range. The 5 Plus adds 12-inch tyres and a 900W peak motor for comfort. The 5 Max keeps 10-inch tubeless tyres but has the strongest 1000W peak motor and traction control.

Which Xiaomi is best for heavier riders?

Skip the lightest budget models. The Scooter 6, 5 Plus, 4 Ultra and 6 Max carry 120–130 kg with stronger motors, bigger batteries and larger tyres.

Do Xiaomi scooters have puncture-proof tyres?

The 4 Ultra and Pro 4 use DuraGel self-sealing tubeless tyres that reseal small punctures automatically. Other models use standard pneumatic or tubeless tyres.

Is the 6 Max worth AED 2,695?

Yes if you commute long distances or want the most capable Xiaomi: 70 km range, 1100W peak, dual disc brakes, full-body IPX6 and Apple Find My. It’s overkill for short trips.
