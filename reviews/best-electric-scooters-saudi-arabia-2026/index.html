🇸🇦 Quick Legal Note Before You Buy

In Saudi Arabia, e-scooters are legal to ride but only in designated areas — cycle tracks, scooter lanes and approved zones, not public roads or sidewalks. The practical speed ceiling is around 20 km/h (5 km/h in parks), the minimum age is 17, and for ordinary low-power scooters no driving licence is currently required. The 25 km/h-class commuter scooters in this guide fit those limits. The two high-performance models at the end do not — treat them as private/off-road only.

What to Avoid: The Workshop Blacklist

Here’s the part nobody tells you…

Not because we don’t want to — because the parts don’t exist in the region, the makers publish no repair info, and the components match no standard.

🚫 Avoid These

No-name imports under SAR 700 — They look like Xiaomis. They’re not. When the battery management board fails — and it will, in summer — you’ve got a doorstop. No parts, no support, no fix.

“1000W” mystery scooters from marketplace third-parties — Power ratings are often fiction; the real motor is far weaker than the listing claims, and range numbers are invented. Real-world range can fall far short of the listing.

Any brand with no Gulf distributor — If you can’t reach a local service contact when something breaks, you’re on your own.

Used scooters bought unseen from social mediaGet any second-hand scooter inspected before paying.

Where to Actually Buy in Saudi Arabia

✅ Recommended Sources

Noon Saudi & Amazon.sa — Widest selection and usually the best prices on Xiaomi and Segway. Check the listing is the official store or “fulfilled by” the platform, not a random third-party seller — warranty and returns are far cleaner that way.

Jarir Bookstore — Carries Segway and other mainstream scooters in-store across Riyadh, Jeddah and beyond. Great when you want to see the sealed box and have a simple warranty path.

Extra (eXtra) — Another solid Saudi electronics retailer for mainstream models, in-store and online.

Official brand channels (Xiaomi Saudi, Segway) — Warranty is real, returns work, and pricing is often the same as resellers.

Specialty dealers for performance brands — For Kaabo or Dualtron, use an established dealer who stocks and services. If they only sell and never repair, that’s a yellow flag.

⚠️ Reality Check on “Deals” & Prices

SAR prices in this guide are indicative — Saudi pricing moves with promotions, stock and VAT, so always confirm the live figure before buying. And if a social-media seller offers a current Xiaomi 4 Pro for half the going rate, something is wrong: it’s likely fake, water-damaged or has a swapped battery. Below the normal floor price, ask hard questions before paying anyone.

📋 The Bottom Line

For most people in Saudi Arabia: Xiaomi 4 Pro, around SAR 1,799. Best all-rounder — good range, strong heat tolerance, mainstream parts, and well inside designated-area speed limits.

Daily long-distance commuter: Segway MAX G2, around SAR 2,399. The self-healing tyres and honest range justify the extra over twelve months.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is the best electric scooter in Saudi Arabia 2026?

The Xiaomi Electric Scooter 4 Pro (around SAR 1,799) is our top overall pick — best balance of heat tolerance, reliability, mainstream parts and value, and its 25 km/h class fits Saudi’s designated-area limits. For long daily commutes, step up to the Segway MAX G2.

❓ Where can I buy an electric scooter in Saudi Arabia?

Noon Saudi and Amazon.sa for the widest choice and best prices, or Jarir and Extra in-store for easy warranty. Always confirm you’re buying from the official store or platform-fulfilled listing, not a random third party.

❓ How much should I spend?

A reliable entry-level scooter starts around SAR 1,000–1,200 (Xiaomi 4). A strong all-rounder (Xiaomi 4 Pro) is roughly SAR 1,700–1,900. Premium long-range commuters (MAX G2, 4 Ultra) run SAR 2,300–2,700. Prices fluctuate — verify live before buying.

❓ Are these scooters legal to ride in Saudi Arabia?

The mainstream 25 km/h-class Xiaomi and Segway models are legal to ride — but only in designated areas, at the local speed limit, by riders 17+. The high-performance Kaabo and Dualtron are far too fast for public paths and should be treated as private/off-road only. See our full Saudi e-scooter law guide.

❓ Which scooter handles Saudi heat best?

The Segway MAX G2 and Xiaomi 4 Pro — both have well-calibrated battery management for high ambient temperatures. Inland Riyadh summers are brutal, so ride early or late and never charge or store a scooter in direct sun. Avoid no-name budget scooters; their battery management often fails in Gulf heat.
