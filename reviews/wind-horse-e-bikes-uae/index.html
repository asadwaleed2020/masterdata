Quick answer

Wind Horse models sold in the UAE range from small folding and basket bikes to very fast machines listed at 85 km/h. We could not name a best model, because we found no official brand documents, and only one model had a stated load limit. On paper, the 16-inch folder suits short trips with limited storage, and the 650W 27.5-inch suits riders who want a full-size bike and have ground-level storage. Ask the seller for the total load limit, warranty terms and legal category in writing before you pay.

How this guide was made: We compared UAE seller listings checked on 3 November 2026 with figures in IonicRide’s own product database. Our database is an internal record of retailer figures, not independent proof, and we could not reopen those retailer pages for this update. IonicRide did not test these bikes. We make no reliability ranking, because we found no reliable data to support one.

What we could and could not confirm

  • No official brand website or manual found. A Global Sources profile describes a Dongguan, China, maker of folding e-bikes and scooters under the Windhorse name. It lists no brand names and does not prove a link to every product sold here.
  • No UAE distributor confirmed. We found sellers, but no document naming an official distributor or service network.
  • Names are not consistent. You will see “Wind Horse” and “Windhorse”, and the same model name can come with different specifications.

So treat every figure below as a seller claim to confirm for the exact unit you are buying.

Models that need extra caution

  • H3, listed at 85 km/h, and M1, listed at 55 km/h. These are far faster than the 20 km/h and 30 km/h limits that RTA lists for urban and amateur cycling tracks. We do not suggest buying them for public cycle tracks. Confirm the vehicle’s legal category first.
  • V8 Pro, listed at 54 km/h. The same caution applies. Other listings we saw also gave different power and speed figures for this name.
  • C2. Dubai’s cycle resolution defines an e-scooter as ridden standing with no seat, so this seated model does not fit that definition. Ask RTA how it is classed before riding in public.
  • 16-Inch, 14-Inch and 650W. Their listed speeds of 35 to 40 km/h are also above those limits. Ask whether the speed can be limited by the manufacturer or seller. Do not use an unlock or an unofficial change.

Read e-bike rules for Dubai cycle paths before choosing. These rules are for Dubai, and other emirates set their own.

A rear seat does not prove it can carry a passenger. A basket does not prove it suits delivery.

Dubai’s 2022 cycle resolution allows an e-bike passenger only if the bike is equipped for one. Ask for the passenger and total load rating in writing. Delivery work has its own rules, so see delivery e-bike rules in Dubai, and check with your employer or platform.

Questions to ask the seller

  • ☐ What is the exact model and version? Is it the one in the photos?
  • ☐ What are the motor power, battery voltage and capacity for this unit?
  • ☐ What is the total load limit, including rider, bag and basket? Where is that written?
  • ☐ What does the warranty cover: frame, motor, battery, controller and display? For how long?
  • ☐ Who handles a claim? Where do I take the bike, and who pays for transport?
  • ☐ Are spare brake parts, tyres, batteries and chargers available?
  • ☐ Which battery and charger safety certificates can you show for this unit?
  • ☐ What legal category does this bike fall under in my emirate? Can you show a document?
  • ☐ What is the return policy if the bike arrives with different specifications?

Keep the replies, the invoice and a copy of the listing. See our e-bike buyer’s guide for other brands to compare.

FAQs

Is Wind Horse a reliable e-bike brand?

We cannot say. We found no independent failure data, official service network or official documents, so ask for strong warranty terms and compare other brands.

Which Wind Horse has the highest load limit?

We cannot rank them. The only total load figure we found was 330 lb, about 150 kg, on one seller’s F2 page.

Does Wind Horse have a UAE warranty?

Sellers advertise different terms. The F2 and M3 pages mention one year for the motor, and the D5 ad mentions one year without details. Get the terms in writing for your bike.

Bottom line

Wind Horse listings offer a lot of power and battery for the listed price, but the public information is thin and sometimes conflicting. If you are still interested, choose a seller who will confirm the exact version, load limit, warranty and support in writing. If they cannot, compare another brand.
