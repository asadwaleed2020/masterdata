Quick answer

If you will carry it often, look first at the Fiido D11 (17.5 kg with battery, confirm UAE supply) or the premium Dahon Unio E20 (18.7 kg, 71 x 42 x 81 cm folded). The ADO A20 is the mid-price option at 21 kg. Folding helps with storage, but it does not make an e-bike easy to carry up stairs, and it does not prove you can take it on the Metro.

How this comparison was made: IonicRide has not ridden, folded or tested these bikes. Specifications come from manufacturer and retailer pages, checked on 3 November 2026. What matters most in a folding e-bike

  • Carried weight. Ask whether the listed weight includes the battery. Only some sources make this clear.
  • Folded size. Measure your lift, boot or storage space. Do not guess from photos.
  • Battery removal. A removable battery can make carrying and charging easier. See our guide to charging in Dubai apartments.
  • Load rating. Include yourself and anything you carry.
  • UAE support. Ask who handles warranty, parts and repairs locally.

Which one suits you?

Fiido D11: lightest in this comparison

CycleSouq lists 17.5 kg with the battery, a removable battery, disc brakes and a 120 kg load. The folded size is compact on paper.

Good fit for: riders who carry the bike regularly and want a mid-range price. Downside: we saw it on CycleSouq’s Qatar page, so confirm UAE delivery, price and warranty first. The seller’s 80–100 km range is a best-case claim.

Dahon Unio E20: premium option

Dahon lists a 200W mid-drive motor with 60 Nm of torque, 9 gears and a removable seatpost battery. Dahon also publishes its folded size clearly.

Good fit for: commuters who pedal, and who value a folding-bike specialist. Downside: the highest price here, and a lower rider limit of 105 kg. The UAE listing shows 32 km/h, so ask whether it can be limited for cycle lanes.

Dahon E-Hemingway: longer and lower when folded

Dahon lists 19 kg, a 250W rear hub motor, mechanical disc brakes, 7 gears and a folded size of 87 x 40 x 72 cm. It is longer than the Unio E20 when folded, but lower.

Good fit for: riders wanting a Dahon at a lower price than the Unio, based on prices in our database. Downside: the same 105 kg rider limit as the Unio, and a retailer’s folded size differs from Dahon’s, so measure before buying.

Check price on Noon

ADO A20: mid-price option

CycleSouq lists a 7-speed Shimano drivetrain, mechanical disc brakes, a removable battery and a 120 kg load.

Good fit for: buyers who mainly want to store it, with less carrying. Downside: about 21 kg is heavy on stairs. Details for the similar A20+ conflict on another UAE listing, including 25 or 35 km/h and a sold-out notice, so confirm the exact version.

Wind Horse 16-Inch: budget, with compromises

This has the lowest price in this comparison, based on our product database, but it is also the heaviest. It lists a 35 km/h top speed, which is above typical cycle lane limits in Dubai.

Good fit for: tight budgets and short trips, if speed can be limited. Downside: small wheels, weight, and missing specs for brakes and folded size. Check these with the seller.

Apartment and carrying reality

  • Stairs: even the lightest bike here weighs 17.5 kg. Try lifting the same weight first.
  • Lifts: measure the depth and the door, then compare with the folded size.
  • Car boots: check the boot with seats up, and protect it from oil on the chain.
  • Heat: avoid storing batteries in a hot car. See how Dubai heat affects e-bike batteries.

Metro and legal checks

Do not assume a folding e-bike can go on the Metro. RTA’s carriage document sets rules for e-scooters, and older reports quote RTA allowing foldable bicycles without batteries. Ask RTA about your exact bike.

Dubai’s Resolution 13 of 2022 sets 16 as the e-bike minimum age, and requires a helmet, reflective vest, lights, a bell and front and rear brakes. It also bans e-bikes from roads above 60 km/h. See e-bikes on Dubai cycle paths.

Checklist before you buy

  • ☐ Weight includes the battery
  • ☐ Folded size fits your lift, boot or storage
  • ☐ Load rating covers you and your bag
  • ☐ Speed setting suits cycle lanes
  • ☐ Lights, bell, reflector and brakes are fitted, or budgeted
  • ☐ UAE warranty and repair contact are clear
  • ☐ Replacement battery and charger are available
  • ☐ Final price includes delivery and assembly

Bottom line

Choose by what you will really do. For regular carrying, start with the Fiido D11 if UAE supply checks out, or pay more for the Dahon Unio E20. For storage with less lifting, the ADO A20 is a reasonable mid-price choice. Confirm the speed setting and local warranty before paying.
