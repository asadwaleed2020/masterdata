How the gold-to-silver ratio works

Divide the international spot price per troy ounce of gold by the same-unit silver price. The ratio is unitless because both quotes use the same currency and weight unit.

It describes relative market prices; it does not predict which metal will rise next. Retail premiums and resale spreads may change the relative value of specific products. Compare Gold UAE and Silver UAE for indicative prices.

Frequently asked questions

Is a high ratio a buy signal?

No. The ratio alone cannot predict returns or tell you whether to buy a metal.

Do the prices include retail spreads?

No. The displayed ratio uses international spot quotes, not dealer purchase and resale prices.
