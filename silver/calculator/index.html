Silver content versus invoice value

Metal content value = weight in grams × fineness × pure silver rate. A shop may instead quote a rate per gram for the actual alloy; use that rate in the invoice calculator without multiplying it by fineness again.

For example, 100 grams of 925 silver contains approximately 92.5 grams of silver. At a hypothetical pure silver rate of AED 5 per gram, its metal content value is AED 462.50. The finished retail price may be higher or lower than a simple metal reference, especially for resale.

Verify tax treatment through the Federal Tax Authority and your invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official UAE retail silver rate?

No. It is international spot data converted to AED. Dealer premiums, making charges and VAT can affect the final price.

How many grams are in one tola?

A bullion tola is approximately 11.6638 grams. A troy ounce is approximately 31.1035 grams.

What is the difference between 925 and 999 silver?

925 silver contains 92.5% silver; 999 contains 99.9%. Jewellery and bullion may have different premiums and resale terms.
