Understanding silver market history

Each historical observation is an international silver wholesale reference in USD per troy ounce, converted into AED per gram. Short ranges may include intraday data; longer ranges use daily observations. Period change is the percentage difference between the first and last available data point.

Prices may be unchanged over market closures. The chart is not a record of retail silver prices in Dubai. See the current silver dashboard for today's indicative quote.

Frequently asked questions

Are historical rates shop prices?

No. These are international wholesale market observations.

Why may the latest chart point differ from the current quote?

History and spot quotes come from different providers and can have different observation times.

Data and methodology

Current international spot quotes from Gold API; historical observations from Standard Bullion. USD prices converted at 3.6725 AED per USD and 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce. Fineness estimates reflect silver content only. Missing or expired quotes are not replaced with fictional values. Confirm actual prices before purchase.
