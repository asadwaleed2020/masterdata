Buying silver in the UAE

Separate the silver content from the final retail price. Compare bar or coin fineness, refiner, dealer premium and buy-back quote. For jewellery, check the net billed weight, alloy, workmanship and all extra charges.

Read our 925 sterling silver guide, 999 fine silver guide or use the silver invoice calculator to compare quotes.

925 sterling silver: what the hallmark means

925 sterling silver is nominally 92.5% silver by mass. The remaining material is an alloy. A 100 g item of that fineness contains about 92.5 g of silver before accounting for non-metal components.

For jewellery, assess workmanship and finished condition. Do not assume the gross weight is the same as silver-alloy weight. Ask the seller for the stated fineness and invoice. Compare 999 fine silver and use the invoice calculator for actual retail quotes.

999 fine silver: bars, coins and fineness

999 silver is nominally 99.9% silver by mass. For a one-kilogram bar, the silver content is approximately 999 grams. A dealer may charge a premium for minting, packaging, brand, distribution or small bar size.

Check the declared fineness, refiner, invoice and buy-back terms. VAT treatment can depend on whether the product qualifies as an investment precious metal. Compare 925 sterling silver for jewellery.

How to compare silver prices in Dubai

Dubai jewellery districts and bullion retailers can quote different prices even for the same silver weight. The international reference shown here is not a Dubai retail fixing. Ask the shop to identify the item's fineness and separately explain the making charge, any stone price and VAT.

For bullion, compare products of equal weight and purity. Ask for an invoice and any authenticity certificate, along with a buy-back quote. For jewellery, compare the finished price per gram rather than only the metal value.

Use the calculator to compare a real pre-tax quote. See Visit Dubai for local shopping information.

How the gold-to-silver ratio works

Divide the international spot price per troy ounce of gold by the same-unit silver price. The ratio is unitless because both quotes use the same currency and weight unit.

It describes relative market prices; it does not predict which metal will rise next. Retail premiums and resale spreads may change the relative value of specific products. Compare Gold UAE and Silver UAE for indicative prices.

Silver content versus invoice value

Metal content value = weight in grams × fineness × pure silver rate. A shop may instead quote a rate per gram for the actual alloy; use that rate in the invoice calculator without multiplying it by fineness again.

For example, 100 grams of 925 silver contains approximately 92.5 grams of silver. At a hypothetical pure silver rate of AED 5 per gram, its metal content value is AED 462.50. The finished retail price may be higher or lower than a simple metal reference, especially for resale.

Verify tax treatment through the Federal Tax Authority and your invoice.

Understanding silver market history

Each historical observation is an international silver wholesale reference in USD per troy ounce, converted into AED per gram. Short ranges may include intraday data; longer ranges use daily observations. Period change is the percentage difference between the first and last available data point.

Prices may be unchanged over market closures. The chart is not a record of retail silver prices in Dubai. See the current silver dashboard for today's indicative quote.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official UAE retail silver rate?

No. It is international spot data converted to AED. Dealer premiums, making charges and VAT can affect the final price.

How many grams are in one tola?

A bullion tola is approximately 11.6638 grams. A troy ounce is approximately 31.1035 grams.

What is the difference between 925 and 999 silver?

925 silver contains 92.5% silver; 999 contains 99.9%. Jewellery and bullion may have different premiums and resale terms.

Is a high ratio a buy signal?

No. The ratio alone cannot predict returns or tell you whether to buy a metal.

Do the prices include retail spreads?

No. The displayed ratio uses international spot quotes, not dealer purchase and resale prices.

Are historical rates shop prices?

No. These are international wholesale market observations.

Why may the latest chart point differ from the current quote?

History and spot quotes come from different providers and can have different observation times.
