UAE labour complaint: unpaid salary, MOHRE & worker rights

Practical steps for wage, leave, gratuity and termination disputes.

Scope: Federal private-sector employment law, principally Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 as amended. DIFC, ADGM, government jobs, domestic workers and pension-covered nationals can have different rules. Contractual benefits may exceed the statutory minimum. Calculators are estimates, not legal determinations.

What to do when salary or employment rights are disputed

Wage Protection System (WPS) in 2026

MoHRE-registered establishments generally pay contractual wages through WPS, subject to recognised exemptions. The UAE government’s current wage-payment guide cites Ministerial Resolution No. 0340 of 2026: wages for the previous month are due on the first day of the Gregorian month. Check current enforcement provisions rather than assuming the older 15-day grace period still applies universally. Official wage payment guidance.

1. Collect your contract, payslips/WPS records, leave certificates, termination notice, settlement statement and relevant correspondence.

2. Raise the specific issue in writing with HR, identifying the unpaid amount or disputed decision. Preserve the response.

3. Contact MOHRE through its official website/app or labour advisory centre 80084; if in a separate jurisdiction, contact that authority.

4. Follow the ministry's resolution, appeal or court procedure and applicable deadlines. MOHRE has decision-making powers for qualifying claims up to AED 50,000 under the amended law.

5. Avoid paying unofficial intermediaries or sharing sensitive documents with this site. Seek qualified legal assistance if the dispute is complex.

Official portal: MOHRE ↗ · Labour dispute guidance ↗. WPS wage-payment requirements and claim deadlines can change; verify the current rules before acting.
