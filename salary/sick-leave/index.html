UAE sick leave law: 90 days, salary & medical certificate

Understand Article 31 sick pay, probation, medical certificates and the consequences of exhausting leave.

Scope: Federal private-sector employment law, principally Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 as amended. DIFC, ADGM, government jobs, domestic workers and pension-covered nationals can have different rules. Contractual benefits may exceed the statutory minimum. Calculators are estimates, not legal determinations.

45 days
Unpaid

After probation, eligible employees have up to 90 sick-leave days per service year, whether taken together or separately. The first 15 are at full wage, the next 30 at half wage, and the remaining 45 without wage. This is not 90 days of paid salary. Prior sick days in the same service year reduce the remaining entitlement.

Medical certificate and notification

Notify your employer within three days of becoming sick and provide a medical report from a recognised medical authority. Follow the employer's reporting process and retain the medical certificate, leave approval and payslips. During probation, there is no statutory paid sick leave, but an employer may permit unpaid sick leave supported by a medical report.

If you are sick for more than three months

The employer may terminate employment if the statutory sick-leave entitlement has been exhausted and the employee still cannot return. Article 46 restricts termination for medical unfitness before the legally accrued leave is used. This does not mean every termination during leave is automatically unlawful for every other reason. Discuss annual leave, agreed unpaid leave, accommodations or contractual benefits in writing; extra paid months are not automatically guaranteed.

For job-related injuries and occupational illness, different rules apply: see work injury compensation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take 90 days fully paid?

No. Full pay for 15 days, half pay for 30, unpaid for 45, subject to eligibility.

Can I take sick leave during probation?

No statutory paid sick leave; employer may authorise unpaid sick leave on medical evidence.

Can my employer dismiss me after 90 days?

If you exhaust entitlement and cannot return, termination may be permitted; specific facts and Article 46 matter.
