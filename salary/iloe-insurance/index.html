Unemployment insurance is not medical income protection

ILOE provides limited compensation for qualifying involuntary job loss, generally 60% of the average basic salary over the preceding six months, subject to policy limits: AED 10,000 monthly for Category A or AED 20,000 for Category B, for up to three months per claim. At least 12 consecutive months of subscription are generally required. Resignation, disciplinary dismissal and other exclusions can prevent a claim.

Submit an eligible claim within the prescribed 30-day period from termination or applicable dispute resolution, and satisfy the other policy requirements. A medical diagnosis or time away from work does not itself trigger ILOE. If employment ends due to illness, ask the insurer whether the specific termination reason qualifies; do not assume automatic approval.

Apply or verify your policy at ILOE official portal. The scheme is separate from employer-default insurance, which can protect certain unpaid employment dues under its own limits and claim procedures.
