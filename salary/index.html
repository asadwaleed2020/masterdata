Ordinary illness and occupational injury have different income protection. With ordinary illness, the entire 90-day statutory entitlement amounts to 30 full-pay-equivalent days if all leave is unused. Qualifying work injury may involve six months of full wage followed by up to six months at half wage. Medical insurance covers eligible care, not automatic salary continuation.

Frequently asked questions

Does health insurance pay salary during sickness?

Usually no. Health coverage and wage replacement are separate. Check whether a separate disability/income-protection policy exists.

Is gratuity calculated on total salary?

Traditional federal gratuity is based on the last basic wage for eligible employees.

Do these rules apply to DIFC and ADGM?

Not necessarily. They have separate employment regimes; confirm the authority shown on your contract.
