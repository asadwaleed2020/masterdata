Using the AED to INR rate

For transfers to India, confirm the INR amount credited to the recipient and any deductions. Compare quotations for the same delivery method and AED budget; a fee-free offer may still use a less favourable exchange rate.

To estimate a conversion, multiply your AED amount by the reference rate. To convert in the opposite direction, divide your INR amount by the rate. Use the remittance comparison to evaluate actual provider quotes after fees.

Things Indian senders often ask

  • Which account to send to? NRIs commonly use NRE or NRO accounts. They have different tax and repatriation rules, so ask your Indian bank which is right for you.
  • Rate vs fee: some providers advertise zero fees but use a lower rate. Compare the final rupee amount.
  • Timing: transfers on Indian bank holidays or late in the day may be credited the next working day.

Send safely

  • Use a bank or a CBUAE-licensed exchange house or app. Informal hawala is only legal through providers registered with the Central Bank.
  • Compare the amount your family receives, not just the rate or the fee.
  • Double-check the recipient's name exactly as on their ID or bank account.
  • Keep your receipt and transaction reference until the money arrives.
  • Never send money to someone you only know online, or because of a call asking for urgent payment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the AED to INR rate today?

The live reference rate is shown at the top of this page. Banks and exchange houses quote their own rate.

What details are needed to send money to India?

The account holder's name, account number and the bank's 11-character IFSC code.

Should I send money to an NRE or NRO account?

They have different tax and repatriation rules. Ask your Indian bank which account suits the money you are sending.

When is the best time to send money to India?

No one can predict daily moves. Use the history chart to see recent ranges, and compare provider quotes on the day you send.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
