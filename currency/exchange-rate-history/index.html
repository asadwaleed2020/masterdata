How to interpret the chart

Select the foreign currency and date range. Each point represents the provider's dated reference observation in foreign units per AED. Period change compares the first and last available rate. A rise means one AED converts to more of that currency, before fees.

Missing days are not filled with made-up prices. For the current reference rate and reverse conversion use the currency dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Can the history chart predict tomorrow's rate?

No. It shows past reference rates only. Use it to see recent ranges, not to forecast.

Why are some days missing?

Reference rates are not published on weekends and some holidays.

Why is the AED to USD line flat?

The dirham is pegged at 3.6725 per US dollar.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
