Frequently asked questions

Are these the actual exchange-house rates?

No. The figures are reference exchange rates. Banks and exchange houses may quote a different rate and may charge additional fees.

How often are exchange rates updated?

Frankfurter publishes reference observations once or a few times a day depending on the data source. Each pair displays its observation date; market holidays can delay updates.

What if the feed is unavailable?

The tool displays unavailable data rather than fabricating a conversion. The remittance calculator still accepts your actual quotes.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
