How we calculate the recipient amount

Subtract the sending fee from your total AED budget. Multiply the remaining amount by the quoted destination currency rate. Subtract any receiving deductions. Effective rate = net amount received ÷ total AED budget.

Example: AED 1,000 budget, AED 10 sending fee, hypothetical 25 foreign units per AED and no receiving deductions gives 24,750 foreign units. Compare that result against the second quote for the same budget.

Check the provider's licensing and payment terms. The calculator does not execute transfers, recommend a provider or guarantee rates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I include a receiving fee?

Yes. Enter it in the destination currency, not AED.

Does the calculator transfer my money?

No. It only calculates and compares the quotes you enter. No payment or account information is collected.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
