Using the AED to PKR rate

For transfers to Pakistan, compare the amount credited to the recipient in PKR rather than only the advertised rate. Check whether the quote is for a bank account, cash pickup or another delivery method, and ask about recipient charges.

To estimate a conversion, multiply your AED amount by the reference rate. To convert in the opposite direction, divide your PKR amount by the rate. Use the remittance comparison to evaluate actual provider quotes after fees.

Pakistan remittance rules in 2026

  • Free for the family: the State Bank of Pakistan stopped reimbursing banks for transfer charges from 1 July 2026, but said eligible home remittances must continue to be free of cost for both senders and beneficiaries. Eligible transfers are generally USD 200 or more.
  • Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme (September 2026): a beneficiary who receives home remittances of USD 100 or more each month for three consecutive months in a quarter, into a bank account, can enter a prize scheme run by the banking industry.
  • Your UAE provider may still charge its own fee or margin. Ask whether your transfer qualifies as fee-free.
Tip: Sending USD 200 or more (about AED 735) in one transfer, instead of several small ones, can help it qualify for fee-free handling.

Send safely

  • Use a bank or a CBUAE-licensed exchange house or app. Informal hawala is only legal through providers registered with the Central Bank.
  • Compare the amount your family receives, not just the rate or the fee.
  • Double-check the recipient's name exactly as on their ID or bank account.
  • Keep your receipt and transaction reference until the money arrives.
  • Never send money to someone you only know online, or because of a call asking for urgent payment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the AED to PKR rate today?

The live reference rate is shown at the top of this page. Your exchange house or bank will quote its own rate, usually slightly lower.

Is sending money to Pakistan free?

The State Bank of Pakistan says eligible home remittances, generally USD 200 or more, must stay free of cost for senders and beneficiaries. UAE providers may still apply their own charges or margin, so check the quote.

What details do I need to send money to a Pakistani bank account?

The recipient's full name as on their CNIC and their 24-character IBAN starting with PK.

Can I send money to JazzCash or Easypaisa from the UAE?

Many UAE exchange houses and apps support mobile wallet payouts in Pakistan. You need the recipient's registered mobile number.

What is the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme?

A prize scheme launched in September 2026 for beneficiaries who receive USD 100 or more each month for three consecutive months in a quarter into a bank account.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
