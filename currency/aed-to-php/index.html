Tips for sending to the Philippines

  • Wallet and cash pickup fees can differ a lot between providers. Compare the pesos received.
  • Check daily limits on the recipient's e-wallet, especially for larger amounts.
  • Around Christmas and school-fee season, send a few days early to avoid queues and delays.

Send safely

  • Use a bank or a CBUAE-licensed exchange house or app. Informal hawala is only legal through providers registered with the Central Bank.
  • Compare the amount your family receives, not just the rate or the fee.
  • Double-check the recipient's name exactly as on their ID or bank account.
  • Keep your receipt and transaction reference until the money arrives.
  • Never send money to someone you only know online, or because of a call asking for urgent payment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the AED to PHP rate today?

The live reference rate is shown at the top of this page. Providers quote their own rate.

Can I send money to GCash from the UAE?

Many UAE exchange houses and apps can send to GCash and Maya. You need the recipient's registered mobile number and name.

What is the cheapest way to send money to the Philippines?

It depends on the day's rate and fee. Compare the peso amount received from two or three providers.

Do Philippine banks use IBAN?

No. You need the bank name, account number and account name.

Data and methodology

Currency UAE uses Frankfurter blended daily reference rates. Cross-currency conversions use each currency's rate relative to AED; observation dates can differ and the converter shows both dates if relevant. Rates are not quotes for trading or money transfers. For official reporting use the required authority's rate. No personal financial information is sent to the data provider.
