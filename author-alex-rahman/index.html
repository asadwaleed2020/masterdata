Why IonicRide publishes under a pen name

Plenty of independent publishers write under a house byline rather than a personal one. IonicRide does the same. It keeps the focus on whether the information is correct and well sourced, rather than on a personality.

The trade-off is that a pen name cannot carry personal credentials, so we do not claim any. You will not find a biography, a job history, or professional qualifications attached to this byline, because there are none to verify. What we offer instead is a documented method, named sources, and a correction process you can hold us to.

02 — Method

How IonicRide articles are researched

Where an article is not based on personal testing, it is desk research, assembled carefully and cited where it matters. That means:

01

Manufacturer documentation

Specifications, owner manuals, and published safety or warranty information from the maker.

02

UAE retailer listings

Prices and local availability from established UAE retailers. Prices move, so we date them.

03

Official sources

Transport authority and government publications for anything about rules, permits, or fines.

04

Owner-reported patterns

Recurring faults reported by owners and repairers, described as reports rather than our findings.

Where a claim comes from one of these sources, we say so. Where information is time-sensitive, such as a price or a regulation, we encourage readers to confirm it directly before acting on it.

03 — Limits

What we do not claim

Being clear about the edges of what we know is more useful to a buyer than an inflated claim, so here are ours:

IonicRide does not operate a workshop or repair facility

We do not run a repair workshop, hold customer repair records, or claim to own every product we write about. We do not sell scooters or e-bikes, and the pen name does not imply an engineering qualification, a manufacturer role, or any government affiliation. Where an article describes a fault pattern we have not encountered first-hand, it draws on documentation and on owner and repairer reports, and it says so. Read the full Editorial Policy for how we handle testing language, AI assistance, and corrections.

04 — Review criteria

The four questions every guide tries to answer

Specifications are a starting point, not a verdict. For a UAE reader, the useful questions are practical ones.

01

How does it handle the heat?

Battery behaviour, thermal cut-offs, and motor stress matter more here than in a temperate climate.

02

Can it be repaired locally?

A model with no parts support in the UAE can cost more over time than a pricier one that is serviceable.

03

Is the stated range realistic?

Rider weight, temperature, tyre pressure, and terrain all pull real range below the advertised figure.

04

Is it legal for the route?

Speed and power limits, permit requirements, and where riding is allowed vary across the emirates.

05 — Q&A

Questions readers ask

Is Alex Rahman a real person?

Alex Rahman is a pen name rather than a legal name, and a real person writes and edits under it. We disclose this so the byline is not mistaken for a named expert whose qualifications you could look up.

Do you physically test the products you write about?

Some of them, yes. Certain e-scooters and e-bikes are personally tested, and those articles say so and describe the first-hand experience. Most guides and comparisons are research-based instead, and those are never written in testing language. The two are always labelled differently.

Do affiliate links influence your rankings?

No. Recommendations are based on documented specifications, local availability, repairability, value, and rider suitability. Commercial links do not buy placement. The details are on our Affiliate Disclosure page.

Do you use AI to produce articles?

Our Editorial Policy sets out how AI assistance is used and reviewed. Every published article is checked by a person before it goes live.

How do I report an error?

Send it through the contact page. We review credible correction requests, update the article, and note significant changes.
