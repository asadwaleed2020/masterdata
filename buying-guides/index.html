FAQ

Buying FAQ

What is the best electric scooter to buy in Dubai 2026?
For most riders the Xiaomi 4 Pro (~AED 1,849) is the best all-rounder — light (14.4 kg), ~32 km usable range, and easy-to-find parts in the UAE. On a budget, the Xiaomi 4 Lite (~AED 877) is strong value; for longer commutes, the Segway MAX G2 (~AED 2,750) adds range.
How much should I spend?
AED 800–1,500: entry-level, short trips. AED 1,500–2,500: the commuter sweet spot. AED 2,500–5,000+: long range and premium build for heavy daily use.
Do I need an RTA permit?
Yes — a free RTA permit is required to ride in Dubai's 21 designated zones. Here's the 15-minute application. Riding without one, or outside zones, carries AED 200–500 fines.
Which specs matter most in UAE heat?
Water/dust protection (IP54+), enough battery (heat cuts real range 25–35%), 250–350W motor for flat commutes, weight under 15 kg for portability, and the right tyre type.
Where can I buy with warranty?
Noon.com and Amazon.ae offer the best prices and fast UAE delivery, and major brands include a 1-year warranty. For repairs and service, see our Dubai repair shop directory.
