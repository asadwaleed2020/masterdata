When to Reset — and When It Won't Help

A reset is a software fix. It solves software problems. It cannot fix hardware problems — and most of the things that go wrong with electric scooters in Dubai are hardware. Knowing which category your problem falls into saves you time and prevents the frustration of resetting a scooter three times and watching the same problem reappear.

⚠️ Before Any Reset — Three Checks
  • Battery must be at least 30% charged. A reset interrupted by a flat battery can corrupt firmware. If the scooter battery is low, charge it first.
  • Try a soft reset first. Hold power button 5–10 seconds until display goes off. Wait 30 seconds. Power back on. This fixes most temporary glitches without wiping settings.
  • The scooter must NOT be locked, charging, or in the middle of a ride when you attempt a factory reset. Most reset procedures will fail or behave unexpectedly in these states.

Where Is the Reset Button on an Electric Scooter?

This is the question that sends most people down the wrong path, so let's clear it up: most modern branded electric scooters do not have a physical reset button at all. On a Xiaomi, Segway-Ninebot or Navee, the "reset button" is the button combination or the app procedure in the brand sections below — there's no separate hidden button to find. Manufacturers moved away from physical reset buttons years ago in favour of control-combo and app resets.

Where a physical reset button does exist, it falls into three cases:

⚠️ Safety First If You're Looking for a Button
  • Power the scooter fully OFF and, on key models, remove the key before touching anything.

  • If you can't find any button, your scooter almost certainly doesn't have one — use the app or the control-combo method for your brand instead.

Hardware Reset Dualtron — BMS / Controller Hard Reset (Deck Power Switch)

Most Dualtron models have a main power switch on the deck (near the charging port) that connects directly to the battery. This enables a true hardware reset:

  1. 1Use the handlebar power button to turn the scooter OFF
  2. 2Locate the deck-mounted main power switch — a physical button or rocker switch on the side or underside of the deck near the charging port
  3. 3Press/flip this switch to cut battery power completely
  4. 4Wait 3–5 minutes — this allows all capacitors to fully discharge
  5. 5Restore the deck switch, then power on normally via the handlebar button
✅Scooter powers back on from a completely cleared state. This resolves most controller errors, throttle calibration issues, and persistent error codes that app-level resets don't clear.
⚠️ Important for High-Performance Models

For Dualtron Thunder 2, Thunder 3, and similar high-power dual-motor models: the deck switch disconnects both battery packs simultaneously. After reconnecting, the BMS performs a self-test on power-on. Allow the display to fully initialise before riding (30–45 seconds).

Soft Reset Dualtron — Soft Restart
  1. 1Hold the power button until the display goes dark
  2. 2Wait 30–60 seconds
  3. 3Power back on normally
✅Resolves most temporary display glitches, Bluetooth drops, and minor error codes. Settings preserved.

Mobility Scooters
Pride Go-Go · Go-Go Elite · iScooter mobility · Drive · most 3/4-wheel
Circuit-Breaker Reset Mobility Scooters — The Reset Button IS a Circuit Breaker
Pride Go-GoGo-Go Elite TravelleriScooter mobilityDrive / generic 3–4 wheel

Unlike stand-up e-scooters, mobility scooters do have a real physical reset button — but it's a resettable circuit breaker, a safety feature that trips (pops out) when the motor or wiring is overloaded, not a settings reset. If your mobility scooter suddenly won't power on, this is the first thing to check.

  1. 1Turn the scooter OFF and remove the key
  2. 2Find the reset button — usually on or near the battery pack / tiller base, sometimes under the seat or on the rear shroud. It's a small button (often red or black) that will be sticking out if it has tripped
  3. 3Press it firmly back in until it stays flush
  4. 4Wait a minute, insert the key, and power on normally
✅If the button stays in and the scooter powers on, the breaker has reset. Give the battery a full charge before your next long trip.
⚠️ If the Breaker Keeps Tripping

A breaker that pops straight back out is telling you something — an overload (too much weight, a steep hill, a stalled motor) or an electrical fault. Don't tape it in or keep forcing it. If it trips repeatedly on flat ground, stop and have the motor, wiring and battery checked by a service centre.

Generic and no-name scooters don't have consistent reset procedures. These are the methods to try in order:

  1. 1Soft restart first: Hold power button 5–10 seconds → wait 30 sec → power on
  2. 2Button combination attempt: Turn OFF → hold Power + Throttle + Brake simultaneously for 3–5 seconds → release when beep or display flash
  3. 3If no response to step 2: turn ON first, then hold Power + Throttle + Brake → wait for beep

⚠️ The Real Problem with Generic Brand Resets

If none of the above works: generic and unbranded scooters frequently have controller firmware that doesn't support user-initiated resets at all. The manufacturer did not implement a reset pathway. In this case, no combination of button presses will achieve a factory reset — the only option is a workshop controller replacement, which on a generic scooter often costs more than the scooter is worth.

How to "Reset the Battery" (BMS) or Controller

People search for a way to "reset the battery" when range drops or the scooter behaves oddly — so let's be straight about what that can and can't do. There are really two different things hiding behind this phrase.

✗ What a Reset CANNOT Do

It cannot restore lost range. If your scooter used to do 40 km and now does 25, that's battery cell degradation — a physical, permanent change no reset can undo. No button combo, app reset, or BMS reset brings capacity back. If range has genuinely declined, the battery needs a health assessment, not a reset.

✓ What a BMS / Controller Reset CAN Fix

A hardware power-cycle of the Battery Management System (BMS) and controller can clear: a stuck or wrong battery-percentage reading, a controller that's thrown a communication error, throttle calibration glitches, and some persistent error codes that an app-level reset won't shift. It's the "unplug it, count to a hundred, plug it back in" of scooters.

Hardware Reset BMS / Controller Reset — The Safe Method
  1. 1Turn the scooter OFF and unplug the charger
  2. 2If your scooter has a deck-mounted power switch (many Dualtron and higher-power models do — a rocker or button near the charging port that connects straight to the battery): switch it OFF to cut battery power completely
  3. 3Wait 3–5 minutes — this lets the capacitors fully discharge and the BMS drop out of its current state
  4. 4Restore the switch (or, on scooters without one, simply leave it fully off for the 3–5 minutes) and power on normally
  5. 5Let the display fully initialise (30–45 seconds on dual-battery models) before riding
✅The scooter powers back from a fully cleared state — clearing many controller errors, false battery readings and calibration issues that survive a normal restart.
⚠️ Do NOT Open the Battery Pack

A BMS reset means power-cycling from an external switch or the main power button — never opening the battery casing or disconnecting cells yourself. Lithium packs are dangerous when opened, and it voids any warranty. If an external power-cycle doesn't clear the fault, it's a workshop job.

After the Reset — What to Do Next

A factory reset returns the scooter to out-of-box state. Every setting you had configured needs to be set up again. Here's the standard post-reset checklist:

✓ Post-Reset Checklist
  • Re-pair with the app: Open your companion app, delete the old scooter entry, and add it as a brand new device. Bluetooth pairing information was wiped by the reset.
  • Set your preferred speed mode: The scooter resets to Standard or D mode. If you use Eco for daily commutes, re-enable it in the app or via the display.
  • Reconfigure speed limit if applicable: Custom speed limits set through the app are wiped. Set them again to your preferred level.
  • Re-enable ambient lighting settings: On models with configurable ambient lighting (Navee GT3, Xiaomi 5 Max), these are reset to default.
  • For Navee GT3 series — re-pair Apple Find My: Delete from Find My app, then enter pairing mode (brake + accelerator 3 times) and re-connect.
  • Test throttle and braking: After a reset, spend 2–3 minutes on a safe flat surface confirming throttle response and brake feel are normal before commuting.

Electric Scooter Reset — Complete FAQ

How do I reset my Xiaomi electric scooter?

Xiaomi 4 series (4 Lite, 4 Base, 4 Pro, 4 Ultra): Turn ON → hold brake lever → press accelerator 5 times → beep confirms reset. 4 Pro 2nd Gen: Turn ON → hold brake + accelerator + power button for 7 seconds → beep. M365/legacy: Turn OFF → hold Power + Throttle + Brake simultaneously 3+ seconds → beep + red "14" on display. App method: Xiaomi Home App → 3-dot menu → Settings → Restore Factory Settings → OK.

How do I reset my Segway Ninebot scooter?

ES and MAX series (G30, G2): Turn ON → hold throttle + brake fully → turn scooter OFF → release both → turn ON (hold power 3 seconds). App method: Segway-Ninebot App → device Settings → Factory Reset. G2 frozen power button: Press and hold power button for 10 seconds to force restart.

How do I reset my Navee GT3 scooter?

Turn ON → confirm NOT locked and NOT charging → squeeze brake lever → press accelerator 5 times. The display turns off to confirm reset. After reset: delete from Apple Find My app and re-pair by holding brake + pressing accelerator 3 times to enter Find My pairing mode.

Where is the reset button on my electric scooter?

Most modern branded e-scooters (Xiaomi, Segway-Ninebot, Navee) have no physical reset button — the reset is a button combination or an app procedure. Some budget or older scooters have a small recessed button near the charging port, under the deck, or in the battery compartment, sometimes needing a pin. Mobility scooters (Go-Go, Pride) do have a physical button — a resettable circuit breaker on or near the battery pack that you press back in after it trips.

How do I reset my electric scooter battery?

You can't "reset" a battery to restore lost range — reduced range is permanent cell degradation, not a software setting. What you can do is a BMS/controller reset: turn the scooter off, switch off the deck-mounted power switch if it has one (or leave it fully off), wait 3–5 minutes for the capacitors to discharge, then power back on. This clears wrong battery-percentage readings and controller errors. Never open the battery pack yourself.

What does a factory reset do to my scooter?

Restores all settings to manufacturer defaults, clears all paired Bluetooth devices, clears error logs, resets speed modes and speed limits, clears PIN/password locks. Does NOT erase firmware, change serial number, or wipe cloud-stored ride history. After reset you will need to re-pair the scooter with your companion app and reconfigure all custom settings.

Will a reset fix my scooter's error codes?

Sometimes. Error codes from software glitches, Bluetooth communication errors, and sensor calibration issues can clear with a factory reset. Error codes from physical hardware failures (damaged motor, faulty battery, broken sensor wire) will reappear after reset because the hardware fault still exists. Try the reset first; if the same code reappears immediately, it's a hardware issue requiring a workshop visit.

What's the difference between a soft reset and a factory reset?

Soft reset (power button hold → off → wait → on): clears temporary glitches without changing any settings or unpairing devices. Factory reset: wipes all custom settings, clears all paired Bluetooth devices, returns to out-of-box state. Always try the soft reset first — it fixes most issues without any loss of configuration.

My scooter won't connect to Bluetooth after reset — what do I do?

After a factory reset, the scooter unpairs from all devices. Open your companion app, delete the old scooter entry, and add it as a brand new device. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone, you're within 2 metres of the scooter, and the scooter is powered on and not in lock mode. If still not visible, try a soft restart of the scooter before re-pairing.

How do I reset my Inokim scooter?

Inokim does not have a single button-combination factory reset. Access display settings by pressing + and − simultaneously for 2 seconds. Navigate modes and restore individual settings manually. For a soft restart: hold power button until display goes off, wait 30 seconds, power back on. For a full controller-level factory reset, contact your Inokim dealer — this requires dealer-level access.

How do I reset my Augment scooter?

Augment doesn't publish a consumer button-combination factory reset. Use a soft restart first (hold power until the display goes off, wait 30 seconds, power on), and for Bluetooth issues delete the scooter from your phone and the Augment app, then re-pair. Restore speed mode, speed limit and lighting individually via the display or app. A full controller-level reset requires dealer/service access — there is no verified secret button sequence.

How do I reset a mobility scooter (Go-Go / Pride)?

Mobility scooters use a physical circuit-breaker reset button, usually on or near the battery pack or tiller base. If the scooter won't power on, the breaker may have tripped and popped out. Turn the scooter off, remove the key, press the button firmly back in until flush, then power on. If it keeps tripping on flat ground, that's an overload or fault needing service — don't force it.

Will a reset fix my scooter's reduced range?

No — in almost all cases. Reduced range is caused by battery cell degradation, not software settings. A reset cannot restore battery capacity. What a reset can fix is an incorrectly configured speed mode set to Sport (which draws more power and reduces range). If genuine range decline is the issue, see our battery range troubleshooting guide.

How much battery do I need before resetting my scooter?

At least 30% charge. Below this, the scooter may not complete the reset process — which in rare cases can leave firmware in a partially reset state. Charge to 30%+ before attempting any factory reset.

How do I reset a scooter bought from a previous owner (locked)?

A factory reset clears PIN locks and Bluetooth pairing from previous owners. Use the button combination for your specific model (see brand sections above). After reset, the scooter is clean and ready to pair with your own app account. Note: for Navee GT3 models with Apple Find My, the previous owner needs to remove the scooter from their Find My account, or you will need to perform the Find My re-pairing procedure after the factory reset.

Segway-Ninebot ES/MAX: ON → hold throttle + brake → turn OFF → release → turn ON (hold 3 sec)

Navee GT3 series: ON (not locked/charging) → hold brake → accelerator ×5 → display off

Inokim: No single-button reset — use display settings menu (+ and − held 2 sec) or contact dealer

Augment: No button-combo reset — soft restart, re-pair Bluetooth, or dealer for a full reset

Dualtron: Minimotors app → Settings → System Settings, or deck power switch off → wait 3 min → on

Mobility scooters (Go-Go/Pride): Press the tripped circuit-breaker button on the battery pack back in

Always try a soft restart first — hold power 5–10 sec, wait 30 sec, power on. This fixes most issues without wiping settings.
