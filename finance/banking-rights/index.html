UAE banking rules, fees & complaints

Your rights before you borrow

  • Ask for a Key Facts Statement and a written offer before you sign.
  • Check the rate type (fixed or variable), total payments, all fees and early settlement terms.
  • Insurance is a separate product. You can choose to pay it upfront or with the loan.
  • Banks may only take post-dated cheques covering the instalments, with a value not exceeding 120% of the loan or debit balance.

Conventional vs Islamic finance

Islamic products may use sale, lease or partnership structures with profit instead of interest, and takaful instead of insurance. Compare the total payments and terms rather than assuming they match a conventional loan.

How to complain: bank first, then Sanadak

  1. Submit a formal complaint to your bank and keep the reference number.
  2. Wait for the bank's written reply. Since October 2025 the bank stage is 15 calendar days.
  3. If you are not satisfied or get no reply, file with Sanadak, the UAE financial ombudsman. Filing the first complaint is free.
  4. Sanadak acknowledges complaints within 3 business days.
  5. Either side can appeal a Sanadak decision within the published deadline.

Sanadak may reject a complaint if you did not complain to the bank first, if it is already in court, or if it is a duplicate. Phone: 800 SANADAK (800 72 623 25).

Suspected fraud? Call your bank's official number immediately. Do not wait for the complaint process. Never share OTPs or passwords.

Frequently asked questions

How do I complain about a bank in the UAE?

Complain to the bank first and keep the reference. If you get no satisfactory reply within 15 calendar days, file a complaint with Sanadak, the UAE financial ombudsman.

Is it free to complain to Sanadak?

Yes, filing an initial complaint with Sanadak is free. Fees can apply to appeals.

What is the maximum late fee a UAE bank can charge?

Caps depend on the product: AED 200 for consumer loans, AED 500 for car loans, AED 230 for credit cards and AED 700 for home loans, excluding VAT.

Can a bank charge me to close my account?

Yes, but the Central Bank caps the account closure fee at AED 100.

What is a Key Facts Statement?

A short document that summarises the product's rate, fees, payments and key terms. Ask for it before you sign so you can compare offers.
