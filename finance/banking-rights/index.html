UAE banking rules, fees & complaints

Understand key facts statements, fees, variable rates, Islamic finance and Sanadak complaints.

Your rights when taking a loan

Request a Key Facts Statement and written offer. Check the initial interest or profit rate, fixed or variable basis, total expected payments, fees, early settlement calculation and consequences of missed payments. A variable rate may change with its benchmark or contract terms. Ask about insurance or takaful and whether it is required.

Conventional versus Islamic finance

Islamic products may use sale, lease or other Sharia-compliant structures with different ownership, profit, rebate and takaful rules. Compare actual total payments and terms rather than assuming the legal or financial structure is identical to conventional interest.

How to complain about a bank or insurer

Submit a formal complaint directly to the institution first and save its reference and written response. Sanadak's eligibility checker asks whether 15 calendar days have elapsed and no satisfactory written response has been received. Check all other eligibility conditions, including jurisdiction, before submitting a complaint.

For suspected fraud, contact your bank immediately using official contact details; do not wait for the ordinary complaint process. Never disclose OTPs, passwords or remote-access codes.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
