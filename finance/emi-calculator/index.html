How EMI is calculated

Monthly rate r = annual nominal reducing rate divided by 12 and 100. For principal P and n monthly installments, payment = P × r / (1 − (1 + r) to the power −n). At zero interest, payment = P / n. Each month interest is calculated on remaining principal, with the rest reducing the balance.

The schedule uses full-precision internal arithmetic, rounds only for display and includes a final balance adjustment. Upfront fees reduce net proceeds for the effective annual cost estimate. Recurring fees add to cash payments, not principal.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
