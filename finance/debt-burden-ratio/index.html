What counts toward DBR?

DBR measures assessed monthly debt repayments against gross salary and verifiable regular income. Banks consider all relevant financing liabilities, including housing and car loans, credit cards and overdrafts. Card obligations may be assessed using limits or the lender's regulatory methodology, rather than just the minimum payment.

Example: AED 10,000 documented monthly income, AED 1,500 existing repayments and AED 2,000 new installment yields 35%. Living costs still matter. Special retirement and product-specific rules may apply; do not assume 50% is available to every borrower.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
