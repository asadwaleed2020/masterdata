Flat rate vs reducing rate: the difference

A flat rate charges interest on the original loan amount for the whole term. A reducing rate charges interest only on the amount you still owe. Because the balance falls each month, a flat rate is always more expensive than the same number as a reducing rate.

How to compare two offers

  1. Ask each bank for the Key Facts Statement and the APR or effective annual rate.
  2. Convert any flat rate to reducing with this tool.
  3. Compare the total amount repaid including fees, not the monthly payment.
  4. Check whether the rate is fixed or variable, and for how long.
Good to know: Central Bank Regulation 29/2011 says personal and car loans follow the reducing balance method of interest calculation. A flat quote is a way of advertising the cost, so always convert it.

Frequently asked questions

Is 3.5% flat rate good in the UAE?

On a 4-year loan, 3.5% flat is about 6.58% on a reducing basis. Compare that figure with reducing-rate offers from other banks.

Why do banks quote flat rates?

A flat rate looks lower because it ignores the falling balance. Converting it shows the true cost.

How do I convert flat rate to reducing rate?

Work out the monthly payment from the flat quote, then find the reducing rate that gives the same payment. This calculator does it for you.

Which is cheaper, flat or reducing?

At the same percentage, reducing is always cheaper. Compare total repayments to decide between real offers.
