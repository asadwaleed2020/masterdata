Why flat rates are not directly comparable

A flat rate calculates interest against the original principal throughout the term. A reducing rate applies to the outstanding balance. A lower advertised flat percentage can therefore produce a similar or greater cost than a higher reducing percentage. Compare actual installments, total costs and the lender's disclosed annual percentage rate.

The effective annual cost model solves the cash-flow rate and compounds it over 12 months. It may differ from the regulated APR convention and real dated cash flows.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
