Mortgage loan-to-value limits

The CBUAE mortgage framework generally sets a maximum 25-year term and a 50% DBR ceiling, with lender stress tests and other affordability restrictions. Typical maximum LTV for an owner-occupied first property valued at AED 5 million or less is 85% for eligible UAE nationals and 80% for eligible expatriates under the amended rules.

Property typeUAE nationalExpatriate
First owner-occupied home ≤ AED 5m85%80%
First owner-occupied home > AED 5m75%70%
Subsequent / investment property65%60%
Off-plan property50%50%

Limits use appraised value and applicable classification, not necessarily the purchase price. The calculator displays LTV but does not decide eligibility. Enter property registration, valuation, agency and other cash costs manually; they vary. The stressed installment models a rate increase applied to the original principal for the full term, not an actual future reset schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
