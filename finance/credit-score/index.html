Check your UAE credit report

Etihad Credit Bureau provides individual credit reports and scores. A report helps lenders review payment history and obligations; a score is not itself an approval decision. Use the official service or app to access your own information, check recorded debts and payment history and follow the data correction process for discrepancies.

Protect your Emirates ID and authentication details. Do not upload them to this independent calculator. Avoid services promising to erase accurate negative credit history. If information is wrong, use the bureau's correction channels and contact the reporting institution.
