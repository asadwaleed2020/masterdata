Why minimum payments take so long

Many UAE cards charge interest monthly. If you only pay a small percentage of the balance, most of it goes on interest and the balance shrinks very slowly.

Example with an AED 20,000 balance and an illustrative 3.25% monthly rate, no new spending:

Fee caps are maximums set by the Central Bank and exclude 5% VAT. A bank may charge less. Your Key Facts Statement shows the fee that applies to you.

A simple payoff plan

  1. Stop using the card while you repay, or the calculator's timeline will not hold.
  2. Set a fixed monthly payment by standing instruction, above the minimum.
  3. If you have more than one card, pay the highest rate first and minimums on the rest.
  4. Compare a balance transfer or a personal loan at a lower reducing rate, including all fees.
  5. If you cannot pay, contact the bank early and ask for a written hardship plan.

Your card limit also affects your debt burden ratio, and late payments appear on your AECB credit report.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum credit card late fee in the UAE?

The Central Bank cap is AED 230 per late payment, excluding VAT.

How long will it take to pay off my credit card with minimum payments?

It can take many years. In our example, AED 20,000 at 3.25% monthly with a 5% minimum takes about 152 months and costs about AED 51,900 in total.

Is a balance transfer worth it?

It can be, if the total cost including fees and the rate after any promotion is lower than your card. Check the terms in writing.

Does my credit card limit affect my loan eligibility?

Yes. Card repayments must fit within the 50% debt burden limit, and some banks count a share of the full limit.
