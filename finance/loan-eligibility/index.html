Can I get a loan on my salary?

Regulatory maximums are not approvals. The general personal-loan ceiling is 20 times documented monthly income, with a term normally no more than 48 months and combined debt assessed within the general 50% limit. Banks can approve less or decline based on affordability and credit policy.

Check existing obligations and credit report before applying. Salary transfer, residence status, employment length, age and employer policies can differ. A loan without salary transfer may have different conditions. Never pay an unknown intermediary for guaranteed approval.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
