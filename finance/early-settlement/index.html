Early settlement fee caps by product

ProductMaximum early settlement fee
Personal loan1% of outstanding balance, max AED 10,000
Partial payment on personal loan1% of amount paid, max AED 10,000
Car loan1% of outstanding balance
Mortgage (full or partial)1% of outstanding balance or AED 10,000, whichever is less

Fee caps are maximums set by the Central Bank and exclude 5% VAT. A bank may charge less. Your Key Facts Statement shows the fee that applies to you.

You may also move (buy out) your loan to another bank. The early payment fee for that transfer is capped at 1% of the outstanding balance or AED 10,000, whichever is less.

AED 100,000 personal loan at 7% reducing over 48 months (EMI AED 2,394.62). After 24 payments:

Settling saves less the later you do it, because most of the remaining payments are principal. Run your numbers above.

How to close a loan early: step by step

  1. Ask the bank for a dated settlement letter showing principal, interest to date, fee, VAT and how long the figure is valid.
  2. Pay the exact amount before the letter expires.
  3. Get a clearance or no-liability letter.
  4. For a car loan, ask for the NOC to the traffic department. The fee cap is AED 0.
  5. For a mortgage, ask the bank to release the mortgage at the land department.
  6. Check your AECB credit report later to confirm the loan shows as closed.
Leaving the UAE? Settle or agree a plan with your bank before you leave. Unpaid debts can lead to legal action.

Frequently asked questions

What is the early settlement fee for a personal loan in the UAE?

At most 1% of the outstanding balance, capped at AED 10,000, plus VAT, under the Central Bank fee caps.

Is there an early settlement fee on car loans?

Yes, up to 1% of the outstanding balance plus VAT. The car loan cap does not include the AED 10,000 ceiling.

What is the mortgage early settlement fee in the UAE?

1% of the outstanding balance or AED 10,000, whichever is less, plus VAT.

Can I transfer my loan to another bank?

Yes. The Central Bank allows a borrower to move a loan between banks for an early payment fee of up to 1% of the outstanding balance or AED 10,000, whichever is less.

Does early settlement improve my credit score?

Closing a loan and paying on time can help over time. Check your AECB report to make sure the loan is shown as closed.
