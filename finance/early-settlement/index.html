Closing a UAE loan early

Ask for a dated settlement letter showing principal, accrued interest or profit, rebate where applicable, settlement charges, VAT and validity date. CBUAE consumer protection standards contain maximum fee schedules by product; the exact permitted basis differs. Do not assume one cap applies to all personal loans, car finance and mortgages.

Compare the actual settlement amount against remaining contractual payments. Confirm any security release and obtain a clearance letter after closing the loan.

Frequently asked questions

Are these live bank rates?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter your actual quoted reducing or flat rate.

Does an estimate guarantee loan approval?

No. Banks assess verified income, expenses, credit history, employment and applicable regulation.

Can Islamic finance be calculated here?

A fixed installment and reducing-profit model may illustrate some products; actual contracts, profit calculation, ownership, rebates and takaful differ. Use the provider's schedule.
