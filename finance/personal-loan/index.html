UAE personal loan rules at a glance

These limits come from Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) Regulation 29/2011. They are the maximum a bank may offer. Many borrowers are approved for less.

RuleLimitWhat it means for you
Maximum amount20 × monthly salary or incomeAED 12,000 salary → up to AED 240,000, if affordability also passes
Maximum term48 monthsYou cannot spread a personal loan over 5 years
All monthly debt payments50% of gross incomeIncludes car loan, mortgage, cards and overdraft
Interest methodReducing balanceInterest is charged on what you still owe
InsuranceSeparate productYou can choose to pay it upfront or with the loan

Banks may lend below these limits if you already have heavy commitments, lower disposable income or uncertain employment. The regulation says this directly.

Check the fees too. Processing fees are often around 1% of the loan, and banks publish their own minimum and maximum in their schedule of charges. Add it in the "Upfront fees" box to see the true cost.

Fees you can be charged (CBUAE caps)

FeeMaximum allowed
Late payment penaltyAED 200
Early settlement (to another bank or from own funds)1% of outstanding balance, max AED 10,000
Partial payment1% of amount paid, max AED 10,000
Loan cancellationAED 100

Fee caps are maximums set by the Central Bank and exclude 5% VAT. A bank may charge less. Your Key Facts Statement shows the fee that applies to you.

Salary transfer or non-salary transfer?

A salary transfer loan requires your employer to pay your salary into the lending bank. Banks see it as lower risk, so rates are often lower. A non-salary transfer loan lets you keep your salary elsewhere, but the rate is usually higher and approval is stricter.

  • Ask whether the bank needs a letter from your employer to assign your end-of-service gratuity.
  • Ask what happens to the rate if your salary stops arriving (for example, if you change jobs).
  • Ask whether the quoted rate is fixed for the full term or only for the first year.

Before you sign: 6-point checklist

  1. Get the Key Facts Statement and compare it with at least one other bank.
  2. Confirm the rate is reducing, or convert a flat rate with the flat vs reducing calculator.
  3. Add all your current payments to the DBR calculator. Stay well below 50%.
  4. Check your AECB credit report for errors before you apply.
  5. Ask for the early settlement amount rules in writing.
  6. Never pay an agent who promises "guaranteed approval".

Frequently asked questions

How much personal loan can I get on my salary in the UAE?

The Central Bank limit is 20 times your monthly salary or total income. On AED 10,000 that is AED 200,000. Your actual offer also depends on the 50% debt burden limit, your credit report and the bank's own policy, so it can be much lower.

What is the maximum personal loan term in the UAE?

48 months. CBUAE Regulation 29/2011 says a personal loan repayment period must not exceed 48 months.

Can I settle my personal loan early?

Yes. The Central Bank caps the early settlement fee at 1% of the outstanding balance, up to AED 10,000, plus VAT. On a AED 50,000 balance the fee is at most AED 500 plus VAT.

What is the late payment fee on a personal loan?

The Central Bank cap for delayed payment penalty charges on consumer loans is AED 200 per instance, excluding VAT. Late payments are also reported to the credit bureau.

Is the minimum salary for a personal loan AED 5,000?

There is no single legal minimum salary that applies to every bank. Each lender sets its own minimum and employer criteria, so check the bank's product page.

Do I have to buy loan insurance?

Under the Central Bank rules, loans and insurance are separate products. You can choose to pay insurance upfront or spread it over the loan term. Ask what cover is required and its cost.
