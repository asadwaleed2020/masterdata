Frequently asked questions

Are the rates on these calculators live bank offers?

No. Default rates are examples. Enter the rate from your bank's Key Facts Statement for an accurate result.

What is the maximum personal loan in the UAE?

20 times your monthly salary or income, repaid over no more than 48 months, under Central Bank rules.

What is the 50% rule for loans in the UAE?

Your total monthly loan and card repayments at all banks must not exceed 50% of your gross regular income.

Is my data saved?

No. All calculations run in your browser. IonicRide does not receive or store what you enter.
