How Ajman municipal parking works

Ajman Municipality lists parking reservations via its mobile app and SMS 5155. Its SMS guide uses the plate source, category and number as separate fields, for example AJM B 0123. The published SMS instructions describe a one-hour booking and extension by responding to the reminder.

The service also lists VIP tickets at AED 3 for half an hour and AED 6 for one hour. These are not assumed to be the ordinary street tariff. Check the municipal app or parking sign for regular-space prices and operating times.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay parking in Ajman by SMS?

Send your plate source, category and number to 5155, for example AJM B 0123. The SMS books one hour.

How much is VIP parking in Ajman?

Ajman Municipality lists VIP parking at AED 3 for 30 minutes and AED 6 for one hour.

Can I extend my Ajman parking by SMS?

Yes. Reply to the reminder message before your ticket expires, following the municipal instructions.

Can I use Dubai plates in Ajman parking?

Yes, use your plate's emirate code as the source in the SMS format.
