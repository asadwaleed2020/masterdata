How estimates work

Select an exact listed tariff duration. For Dubai, select peak or off-peak according to your actual session; mixed sessions produce a range, not a payable quote. For Abu Dhabi, standard tickets use AED 2/hour or AED 15 for the full-day option; premium tickets have a four-hour limit. Sharjah SMS fees are included when selected.

Ajman estimates apply only to the published VIP tariff, not ordinary parking. Do not add a guessed SMS surcharge.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the parking calculator?

It uses published tariffs. Mixed peak and off-peak sessions, events and private car parks can differ, so confirm in the operator's app.

Which is cheaper: zone C or zone D in Dubai?

Both cost AED 2.10 for one off-peak hour, but D is a parking plot with a day ticket, while C is roadside with a 4-hour limit.

Does the calculator include VAT?

Yes for Dubai, where Parkin adds 5% VAT since 1 June 2026.
