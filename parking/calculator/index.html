How estimates work

Select an exact listed tariff duration. For Dubai, select peak or off-peak according to your actual session; mixed sessions produce a range, not a payable quote. For Abu Dhabi, standard tickets use AED 2/hour or AED 15 for the full-day option; premium tickets have a four-hour limit. Sharjah SMS fees are included when selected.

Ajman estimates apply only to the published VIP tariff, not ordinary parking. Do not add a guessed SMS surcharge.

Frequently asked questions

Is a generated SMS a parking ticket?

No. You must send it to the correct number and receive a successful virtual ticket confirmation.

Is all parking free on Sundays?

No. Standard municipal arrangements differ by emirate and zone. Private parking, special zones and 24-hour areas may still charge.

Can I use this calculator for every parking zone?

No. It covers listed tariff categories and selected municipal services. Check your actual sign and official payment quote.
