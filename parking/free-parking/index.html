Holiday free parking is announced by each emirate before the holiday. In 2026, for example, Dubai made public parking free from 25 to 29 May for Eid Al Adha and on 15 June for the Hijri New Year, while multi-storey car parks stayed paid.

Tips for holidays and Ramadan

  • Wait for the official announcement; do not assume every holiday is free.
  • Paid hours often change in Ramadan. Each emirate publishes Ramadan timings every year.
  • Free days do not allow parking in reserved, resident or people-of-determination spaces.
  • In Sharjah, check for a blue sign: those zones charge on Fridays and holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Is parking free in Dubai on Sunday?

Yes, in normal public parking zones. Multi-storey car parks charge every day.

Is parking free in Sharjah on Friday?

Yes in standard zones. Blue-sign zones are paid every day, including Fridays and holidays.

Is parking free on public holidays in the UAE?

Usually yes in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah standard zones, but each emirate announces the details and exceptions before each holiday.

Is multi-storey parking free on holidays?

Generally no. Dubai and Abu Dhabi keep multi-storey car parks paid during holidays.
