Common reasons for penalties

Unpaid parking, expired tickets, incorrect zones, wrong plate details, using restricted spaces without permits, or exceeding maximum stay. The amount depends on the violation and authority; do not use one city's fine schedule for another.

Disputing a fine

Save your ticket confirmation, fine reference, plate details and photos of the sign if relevant. Submit the evidence through the relevant official dispute channel. Avoid unofficial payment links and do not submit sensitive vehicle records to this informational website.

Before you pay: The parking sign and official payment quote take priority. This is an independent information tool, not a parking operator. A generated SMS is not a ticket; wait for a successful confirmation. Never assume a private or special zone follows municipal rates.

How to dispute a parking fine

  1. Take a screenshot of your payment confirmation as soon as you pay.
  2. Photograph the sign and your car if the zone or markings were unclear.
  3. Open the official app or portal for that emirate and choose the objection or dispute option.
  4. Attach your evidence and keep the reference number.
  5. Do not pay through links sent by unknown numbers; check the fine in the official app first.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fine for not paying parking in Dubai?

AED 150 for not paying or not displaying a ticket, according to the RTA fines list.

What is the fine for overstaying parking in Dubai?

AED 100 for staying after the ticket expires, and AED 100 for exceeding the maximum parking hours.

What is the parking fine in Sharjah?

AED 150 for not paying and AED 100 for staying beyond the paid time.

How do I check parking fines in Abu Dhabi?

Use TAMM or the DARB app with your plate details. Fines are issued electronically.

Can I dispute a parking fine?

Yes. Use the objection service of the operator that issued it and attach evidence such as your payment confirmation.
