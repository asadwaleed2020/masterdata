Frequently asked questions

Is a generated SMS a parking ticket?

No. You must send it to the correct number and receive a successful virtual ticket confirmation.

Is all parking free on Sundays?

No. Standard municipal arrangements differ by emirate and zone. Private parking, special zones and 24-hour areas may still charge.

Can I use this calculator for every parking zone?

No. It covers listed tariff categories and selected municipal services. Check your actual sign and official payment quote.
