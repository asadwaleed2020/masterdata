What to do if parking SMS fails

Check your mobile balance, plate source and category, full zone code, duration and destination number. A failed message or balance deduction alone does not establish that a ticket is active. Use an alternative official payment method and save the confirmation. Do not leave your vehicle unpaid while troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

What number do I SMS for parking in Dubai?

7275. Each message costs an extra AED 0.30.

What is the parking SMS format in Sharjah?

Plate source, plate number and hours, sent to 5566, for example SHJ 12345 1.

Does this tool send the SMS for me?

No. It only builds the message. You send it from your phone.

Is my plate number saved?

No. The generator runs in your browser and nothing is sent to IonicRide.
