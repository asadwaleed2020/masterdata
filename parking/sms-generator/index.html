Before you pay: The parking sign and official payment quote take priority. This is an independent information tool, not a parking operator. A generated SMS is not a ticket; wait for a successful confirmation. Never assume a private or special zone follows municipal rates.

What to do if parking SMS fails

Check your mobile balance, plate source and category, full zone code, duration and destination number. A failed message or balance deduction alone does not establish that a ticket is active. Use an alternative official payment method and save the confirmation. Do not leave your vehicle unpaid while troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Is a generated SMS a parking ticket?

No. You must send it to the correct number and receive a successful virtual ticket confirmation.

Is all parking free on Sundays?

No. Standard municipal arrangements differ by emirate and zone. Private parking, special zones and 24-hour areas may still charge.

Can I use this calculator for every parking zone?

No. It covers listed tariff categories and selected municipal services. Check your actual sign and official payment quote.
