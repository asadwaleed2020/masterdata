Sharjah parking fees and payment

The municipal service lists SMS totals of AED 2.38, 5.38, 8.38 and 12.38 for one, two, three and five hours, respectively. The stated transaction fee is AED 0.38. Private UAE plates can use the published format without registration; non-private and GCC vehicles may need to register. The SMS guide also contains older one-hour wording, so check the confirmation and available durations before relying on a longer ticket.

Sharjah city message example: SHJ 12345 1. Khorfakkan uses an additional KH token: SHJ 12345 KH 1. Note that Abu Dhabi plate source is AUD for this service, unlike some other emirates.

Blue/extended-hours signs can indicate charges on all days including public holidays. Follow the exact posted hours; ordinary parking arrangements are not universal.

Frequently asked questions

What are Sharjah parking timings in 2026?

Since 1 July 2026, paid parking runs from 8 am to midnight. Standard zones are free on Fridays and public holidays; blue-sign zones charge every day.

How much is parking in Sharjah by SMS?

The municipality lists SMS totals of AED 2.38 for 1 hour, AED 5.38 for 2 hours, AED 8.38 for 3 hours and AED 12.38 for 5 hours, including the AED 0.38 fee.

Is parking free in Sharjah on Friday?

Yes in standard yellow-sign zones. Blue-sign zones charge on Fridays and holidays.

What is the fine for not paying parking in Sharjah?

AED 150. Staying beyond your paid time is AED 100.

How do I pay Sharjah parking by SMS?

Send your plate source, number and hours to 5566, for example SHJ 12345 1. For Khorfakkan add KH: SHJ 12345 KH 1.
