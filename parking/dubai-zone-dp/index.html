What zone DP means

Zone DP is a premium parking plot (off-street) tariff in Dubai's Parkin system. D zones are traditionally used for parking lots outside the main commercial areas. The P means a premium space: busy, high-demand bays where peak hours cost more. You can park for up to 24 hours on one ticket.

Your sign shows a full code, such as 365DP. Always enter the full code in the app or SMS, not just the letter.

Where premium parking applies

Parkin classed about 40% of the RTA public parking portfolio as premium when variable pricing began in April 2025, along with about 35% of spaces run for private developers. Premium bays are in high-demand, densely populated places, including spaces near public transport such as metro stations. Dedicated signs mark them.

  • Peak premium price: AED 6.30 per hour, about 50% more than standard D.
  • Off-peak, you pay the standard price, so a premium bay is no more expensive from 10 am to 4 pm and 8 to 10 pm.
  • If a standard D bay is free nearby during peak hours, it is cheaper for the same stay.

At peak times (8–10 am and 4–8 pm), a premium DP space costs AED 2.10 more per hour than standard D. Off-peak, premium spaces drop back to the standard price. All prices include 5% VAT, which Parkin added on 1 June 2026.

Day tickets in B and D zones are a fixed price for the whole paid day.

Paying by SMS to 7275 adds AED 0.30 per message. Since 1 June 2026 parking meters no longer take cash, but you can still pay with a nol card or the apps.

How to save money and avoid fines

  • Staying most of the day? Compare the day ticket with hourly tickets; for long stays the day ticket is usually cheaper.
  • Arrive after 10 am or leave before 4 pm to avoid peak prices.
  • Set a reminder 10 minutes before your ticket ends; renew in the app before it expires.
  • Check the sign for a P. A premium sign means the higher peak price.
  • Sunday and public holiday parking is free in normal zones, but multi-storey car parks still charge.

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking in Dubai zone DP?

One hour costs AED 2.10 off-peak and AED 6.30 at peak times (8–10 am and 4–8 pm), including 5% VAT.

What is the maximum parking time in zone DP?

Up to 24 hours on one ticket in parking plot (off-street) D zones. A day ticket is available.

Is there a day ticket in zone DP?

Yes. A full-day ticket costs AED 31.50 including VAT.

What is the difference between zone D and zone DP?

DP is the premium version of zone D. It costs more during peak hours: AED 6.30 instead of AED 4.20 for one hour. Off-peak, both cost the same.

Is zone DP parking free on Sunday?

Yes, standard public Parkin zones are free on Sundays and official public holidays unless the sign says otherwise. Multi-storey car parks still charge.

What is the fine for not paying parking in Dubai?

AED 150 for not paying or not displaying a ticket, and AED 100 for staying after your ticket expires, according to the RTA fines list.
