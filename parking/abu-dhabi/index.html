MAWAQiF standard and premium parking

Standard spaces use black and turquoise markings; premium spaces use white and turquoise markings. Standard parking is AED 2 per hour or AED 15 for the whole day, with a 24-hour maximum. Premium parking is AED 3 per hour with a four-hour maximum. Ordinary surface parking is charged from 08:00 to midnight, with Sundays and public holidays free under the published rules.

Resident permit restrictions

Resident-only spaces within standard zones are restricted to eligible permit holders from 9 PM to 8 AM. Villa resident parking is reserved for permit holders all day unless an eligible visitor permit applies. Free hours do not override these restrictions.

Parking fines in Abu Dhabi

MAWAQiF fines depend on the violation, and resident-permit areas are strictly enforced at night. Fines are now issued electronically and sent by SMS, so you may not see a paper slip.

  • Check and pay fines in TAMM or the DARB app using your plate number.
  • If you think a fine is wrong, use the objection option in the same service and attach your payment proof or photos.
  • Never pay through links received from unknown numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking in Abu Dhabi?

Standard MAWAQiF parking is AED 2 per hour or AED 15 for the day. Premium parking is AED 3 per hour, up to 4 hours.

Is parking free in Abu Dhabi on Sunday?

Yes, standard and premium MAWAQiF surface parking is free on Sundays and public holidays. Resident-permit rules still apply.

What are paid parking hours in Abu Dhabi?

8 am to midnight on paid days.

How do I know if a space is premium in Abu Dhabi?

Premium spaces have white and turquoise kerb markings. Standard spaces have black and turquoise markings.

Can I park in resident spaces at night in Abu Dhabi?

Not without a valid permit. Resident-only spaces are reserved for permit holders from 9 pm to 8 am.
