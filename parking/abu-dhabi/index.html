MAWAQiF standard and premium parking

Standard spaces use black and turquoise markings; premium spaces use white and turquoise markings. Standard parking is AED 2 per hour or AED 15 for the whole day, with a 24-hour maximum. Premium parking is AED 3 per hour with a four-hour maximum. Ordinary surface parking is charged from 08:00 to midnight, with Sundays and public holidays free under the published rules.

Resident permit restrictions

Resident-only spaces within standard zones are restricted to eligible permit holders from 9 PM to 8 AM. Villa resident parking is reserved for permit holders all day unless an eligible visitor permit applies. Free hours do not override these restrictions.

Frequently asked questions

Is a generated SMS a parking ticket?

No. You must send it to the correct number and receive a successful virtual ticket confirmation.

Is all parking free on Sundays?

No. Standard municipal arrangements differ by emirate and zone. Private parking, special zones and 24-hour areas may still charge.

Can I use this calculator for every parking zone?

No. It covers listed tariff categories and selected municipal services. Check your actual sign and official payment quote.
