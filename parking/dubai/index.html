Dubai peak and off-peak parking

Parkin publishes separate peak and off-peak ticket amounts in relevant public zones. The usual peak windows are 8–10 AM and 4–8 PM; the remaining charged hours are off-peak. Many standard zones operate Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 10 PM. Business Bay GP, mosque zones, certain industrial areas and other special facilities can operate on different days or hours.

Multi-storey car parks have a separate round-the-clock tariff. Read the entrance notice. Event parking in the Trade Centre area can have a separate rate.

How to pay public parking in Dubai

Use Parkin, RTA Dubai or Dubai Now where supported, the official portal, parking meter, or SMS to 7275. For an unregistered Dubai private plate, a sample message is A12345 123A 2. The zone must be the full number printed on the sign, not just a letter. A registered nickname may be used according to Parkin's instructions. Every SMS costs an additional AED 0.30; wait for confirmation. To extend, follow the reminder before expiry.

Multi-storey car parks have their own tariff: a base of AED 5 per hour with a daily ceiling of AED 40 (before VAT), and they charge every day.

What changed in 2025 and 2026

  • 4 April 2025: Parkin introduced peak pricing. Premium spaces cost AED 6 per hour (before VAT) at peak times.
  • 1 June 2026: 5% VAT added to all Parkin parking, permits and seasonal cards. AED 4 became AED 4.20 and AED 6 became AED 6.30.
  • 1 June 2026: cash payment at parking meters phased out. Use a nol card, the Parkin or RTA apps, or SMS.

Premium (P) parking: where it applies

When variable pricing began in April 2025, Parkin classed about 40% of the RTA public parking portfolio as premium, plus about 35% of spaces run for private developers. Premium bays are in busy, densely populated places, including near public transport such as metro stations, and are marked with dedicated signs.

  • Premium peak price: AED 6.30 per hour (incl. VAT), about 50% more than standard.
  • Off-peak, premium bays cost the same as standard bays.
  • No P after the letter on your sign means a standard space.

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking in Dubai per hour?

Standard spaces cost AED 4.20 per hour at peak times and AED 2.10 to AED 4.20 off-peak depending on the zone. Premium spaces cost AED 6.30 at peak times. All prices include 5% VAT.

What are peak parking hours in Dubai?

8 am to 10 am and 4 pm to 8 pm, Monday to Saturday. Other paid hours (10 am–4 pm and 8–10 pm) are off-peak.

Is parking free in Dubai on Sunday?

Yes, normal public parking zones are free on Sundays and public holidays. Multi-storey car parks and some special zones still charge.

Can I pay Dubai parking with cash?

Cash at parking meters was phased out from 1 June 2026. Pay with a nol card, the apps or SMS to 7275.

Is VAT charged on Dubai parking?

Yes. Parkin applies 5% VAT to all parking services from 1 June 2026.

How do I send a parking SMS in Dubai?

Send your plate details, zone code and hours to 7275. Use our SMS generator to build the message. Each SMS costs an extra AED 0.30.

What is the maximum parking time in Dubai zones A and C?

Up to 4 hours on one ticket. These are roadside zones; for longer stays use a B or D parking plot or a multi-storey car park.

Is there a day ticket in Dubai zones B and D?

Yes. Day tickets cost AED 31.50 in B, AED 42 in BP, AED 21 in D and AED 31.50 in DP, including VAT.

What does P mean in a Dubai parking zone code?

P marks a premium space, such as AP or BP. Premium spaces cost AED 6.30 per hour at peak times instead of AED 4.20; off-peak they cost the same as standard.

What does the letter in a Dubai parking code mean?

It is the tariff zone. A and C are roadside, B and D are parking plots. The number before it identifies the area.
