Spot price vs the jeweller's price

Spot value is not the jeweller's selling price

International spot gold is commonly quoted in US dollars per troy ounce. We convert the quoted price to AED per gram using 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce and an indicative USD/AED rate of 3.6725. The 22K, 21K and 18K estimates reflect their nominal gold content, not official shop prices. Dubai Jewellery Group publishes suggested retail jewellery rates separately.

Compare like for like at the gold souk

Ask for the same karat, net gold weight, gold rate, making charge and tax treatment when comparing two shops. Stones, clasps and other components may need separate pricing. Request a written breakdown before paying.

How the estimate is calculated

Gold value = weight in grams × shop rate. Add making charges and other taxable charges, then apply the entered VAT percentage to that subtotal. This assumes the entered shop rate is before VAT. A troy ounce and an ordinary household ounce are not the same unit.

22K gold: what it means and how it compares with 24K

22 karat gold nominally contains 22 parts gold out of 24, or about 91.67% gold by mass. Alloying makes it more suitable for many jewellery designs than nearly pure gold. Hallmarks may use 916; confirm the item's actual declared fineness.

Our estimate multiplies the international pure-gold value by 22/24. This does not include retail premiums, craftsmanship or taxes. For a finished piece, compare the full quoted price per net gram using the calculator.

Compare 22K with 24K

22K is common in traditional jewellery, while high-purity bullion has different premiums and resale terms. See our 24K guide for the pure-gold reference.

24K gold: bars, coins and fineness

24 karat describes nearly pure gold, often sold as bars, coins or high-purity jewellery. Products marked 999 or 999.9 have declared fineness expressed in parts per thousand; check the certificate and invoice rather than assuming every item has the same fineness.

The international spot benchmark excludes dealer spreads, fabrication premiums, delivery and tax. Ask for both the purchase quote and the dealer's buy-back conditions. For jewellery with lower purity, see the 22K reference.

Understand bullion pricing

A quoted bar price can differ from the spot value because of its brand, size and distribution costs. Compare bars of the same weight and fineness, and confirm authenticity documentation.

Dubai: buying at Deira Gold Souk

Deira's Gold Souk is a useful starting point for comparing multiple jewellers in a walkable area. The Dubai Jewellery Group reference is particularly relevant here: check the displayed suggested rate, ask for the gold-only weight, and compare the making charge separately rather than judging a piece only by its headline rate.

Before you visit

Decide whether you want everyday jewellery, a gift or bullion. Ask for the karat, weight and stone charges in writing. For coins and bars, request the refiner, fineness and buy-back terms. Keep the invoice.

Read the official Visit Dubai gold-shopping guide for local context.

Abu Dhabi: Madinat Zayed Gold Centre

The Madinat Zayed Gold Centre, beside Madinat Zayed Shopping Centre, brings together established jewellery stores in Abu Dhabi. It is a practical place to compare itemised quotations for plain gold jewellery and pieces with diamonds or pearls. Visit more than one store when comparing making charges.

Compare actual Abu Dhabi quotes

The spot value above is not a separate Abu Dhabi official retail fixing. Ask each shop to specify the gold purity, net metal weight, rate in AED per gram and any making, stone or tax charges. Confirm exchange and resizing policies before paying.

See Experience Abu Dhabi's Madinat Zayed guide.

Sharjah: Central Souk

Sharjah's Central Souk is a well-known shopping area for jewellery and other goods. Compare quotes for similar weights and purities rather than relying on a display price alone. Ask whether the total quoted includes making charges and VAT, and whether stones contribute to the gross weight.

Shopping in Sharjah

For a wedding set or a gift, compare the finished price and craftsmanship, not just the metal reference. Ask for a written invoice with the net gold weight, purity and all extras. Confirm the shop's trade-in and buy-back policies; these are not uniform.

Check the official Sharjah tourism website for local market information before visiting.

What to check on your invoice

Net gold weight, karat, price per gram, making charges, stones, taxes and final amount. Compare the final cost for like-for-like pieces.

How the jewellery calculator works

Gold value = net gold weight in grams × quoted AED per gram. Add the making charge, either a flat amount, per gram, or a percentage of gold value. Add other taxable charges and apply the entered VAT rate to the subtotal.

Example using hypothetical figures: 10 g at AED 400/g plus AED 20/g making charges gives AED 4,200 before VAT. At 5%, VAT is AED 210 and the estimated total is AED 4,410.

Do not enter a tax-inclusive quote and then add VAT again. Investment-grade bullion may qualify for different tax treatment. Review Federal Tax Authority guidance and your invoice.

How to read the gold price chart

Choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or one year. The chart uses timestamped international wholesale gold observations from Standard Bullion and converts USD per troy ounce into AED per gram using 3.6725 AED per USD. The displayed range may contain intraday observations or daily closes depending on the provider.

Period change compares the first and last available observations, not an annualized return. Weekends and market closures may produce unchanged prices. This history is not a record of Dubai Jewellery Group retail price boards.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official UAE retail gold rates?

No. These are international spot prices converted to AED. Dubai Jewellery Group publishes separate suggested retail jewellery prices. Individual shop invoices can include making charges and other costs.

How many grams are in one tola?

A bullion tola is approximately 11.6638 grams. A troy ounce is approximately 31.1035 grams.

What happens if the price feed is unavailable?

The price cards show a dash rather than an invented value. You can still enter a shop quote manually in our calculator.

Why do the chart and today's rate differ?

They use different market providers and may have different timestamps, quote conventions and closing observations.

Can I use this as a history of retail prices?

No. It tracks international wholesale gold spot observations converted into AED, not UAE retail prices.

Does the calculator use official retail rates?

No. You can enter the actual shop quote, or explicitly choose the indicative spot estimate.

Can I calculate in tolas?

Yes. Select tola; the calculator converts to approximately 11.6638 grams per tola.

How are making charges calculated?

Choose per gram, flat AED amount, or a percentage of the metal value. Enter the matching quote.
