Frequently asked questions

Are these official UAE retail gold rates?

No. These are international spot prices converted to AED. Dubai Jewellery Group publishes separate suggested retail jewellery prices. Individual shop invoices can include making charges and other costs.

How many grams are in one tola?

A bullion tola is approximately 11.6638 grams. A troy ounce is approximately 31.1035 grams.

What happens if the price feed is unavailable?

The price cards show a dash rather than an invented value. You can still enter a shop quote manually in our calculator.

Sources and methodology

International spot quotes from Gold API. Gold values use nominal karat ratios; prices converted from USD/troy ounce with 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce and 3.6725 AED per USD. Dubai Jewellery Group publishes separate retail guidance. No prices or trends are fabricated when a feed is unavailable. This is an independent information service, not a jewellery retailer.
