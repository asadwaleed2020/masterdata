Silver prices and purity in the UAE

Silver is quoted internationally in USD per troy ounce. We convert the pure silver reference to AED per gram and scale it by weight. Sterling silver contains 92.5% silver by mass; 999 silver is 99.9% pure. A finished piece may cost more than its metal content due to workmanship and retailer charges.

Ask whether the quote covers net silver weight, fabrication and tax. For gold prices and jewellery calculations, return to the Gold UAE dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the shop price?

No. It is the international silver spot value converted to AED. Retail products may include additional charges.

How do I value sterling silver?

Choose 925 fineness in the calculator and enter the pure silver reference rate. This estimates only the silver content.
