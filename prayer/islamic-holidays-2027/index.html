UAE public holiday entitlements

Eid Al Fitr is 1–3 Shawwal, with 30 Ramadan included if Ramadan lasts 30 days. Arafah Day is 9 Dhu al-Hijjah and Eid Al Adha is 10–12 Dhu al-Hijjah. Hijri New Year and the Prophet's Birthday each have one day under the current holiday resolution. Gregorian dates and observed days may differ following government announcements. Check the UAE government holiday list before making travel or leave arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

When is Eid Al Fitr 2027?

The calendar projects 9–11 March 2027, subject to the official UAE announcement.

When is Eid Al Adha 2027?

The calendar projects Arafah on 15 May and Eid Al Adha on 16–18 May, subject to the official UAE announcement.

Is Ashura a UAE public holiday?

No. It is a religious observance, but it is not included in the current federal holiday list.

Sources and calculation method

Prayer schedules use AlAdhan method 16 (Dubai experimental, Fajr and Isha at 18.2°; standard Asr). This is an independent calculated timetable, not an official UAE authority timetable. Confirm your local prayer and fasting times with UAE Awqaf or Dubai IACAD. City coordinates are approximate city-centre locations, not the whole emirate. Times use Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4. Islamic dates use the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar via AlAdhan; the UAE may confirm different dates through moon sighting. Public holiday entitlements are based on the official UAE government holiday list.
