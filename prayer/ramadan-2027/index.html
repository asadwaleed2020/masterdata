Frequently asked questions

When does Ramadan 2027 start in the UAE?

The calculated Umm al-Qura calendar projects Monday 8 February 2027. Official moon sighting may change the date.

When is Eid Al Fitr 2027?

1 Shawwal is projected for 9 March 2027. The UAE will confirm dates through official announcements.

What is the difference between suhoor, sehri and imsak?

Suhoor or sehri is the pre-dawn meal. Imsak is shown as a precaution before Fajr. Fajr is the start of the fast.

When do Muslims break their fast?

At sunset, at the local Maghrib time. Follow the official local timetable.

Will Ramadan last 29 or 30 days?

The projected calendar has 29 days, but the actual month may have 29 or 30 days based on moon sighting.
