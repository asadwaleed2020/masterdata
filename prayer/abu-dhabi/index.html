Local prayer guidance in Abu Dhabi

The Abu Dhabi city calculation is for the capital, not the whole emirate. Al Ain, Al Dhafra and western settlements can have different dawn and sunset times. Refer to the UAE Awqaf timetable for your actual locality.

All calculations are shown in Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4), with Fajr and Isha calculated using the experimental Dubai method. For iqamah and Friday prayers, refer to your mosque.

How Abu Dhabi prayer times differ from Dubai

Abu Dhabi is west of Dubai, so the sun rises and sets a little later there. Over November 2026, Maghrib (iftar time in Ramadan) is on average 5 min later and Fajr is 3 min later.

Frequently asked questions

What time is Friday prayer in Abu Dhabi?

12:45 pm. Since 2 January 2026, Friday sermon and prayer are held at the same time in all UAE mosques.

Are Abu Dhabi prayer times the same as Dubai?

Not exactly. On average this month, Fajr is 3 min later and Maghrib is 5 min later than in Dubai, based on city-centre calculations.

Are these official Abu Dhabi prayer times?

They are calculated estimates for the city centre. Follow the timetable from your local mosque or the UAE religious authority, especially for Fajr and Maghrib.

What time is Fajr today?

Select your emirate capital and date above to see the calculated Fajr start. The official local mosque timetable takes priority.

Why do prayer times vary across the UAE?

Latitude, longitude, solar position and calculation conventions affect the timings. The same time zone does not mean that dawn and sunset occur at the same minute in every city.

Is Imsak the same as Fajr?

No. The API shows Imsak as a precaution before Fajr. Fajr marks the beginning of fasting; use your local authority's timetable.

Are these official prayer times?

No. This page uses the experimental Dubai method from AlAdhan. Check UAE Awqaf, Dubai IACAD or your local mosque for official times.

What is the Friday prayer time in the UAE?

Awqaf announced a nationwide Friday sermon and prayer time of 12:45 PM starting 2 January 2026. Confirm the latest instructions for your mosque, since this is separate from calculated Dhuhr.

Sources and calculation method

Prayer schedules use AlAdhan method 16 (Dubai experimental, Fajr and Isha at 18.2°; standard Asr). This is an independent calculated timetable, not an official UAE authority timetable. Confirm your local prayer and fasting times with UAE Awqaf or Dubai IACAD. City coordinates are approximate city-centre locations, not the whole emirate. Times use Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4. Islamic dates use the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar via AlAdhan; the UAE may confirm different dates through moon sighting. Public holiday entitlements are based on the official UAE government holiday list.
