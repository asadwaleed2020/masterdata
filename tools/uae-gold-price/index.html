How to use UAE gold prices

Spot value is not the jeweller's selling price

International spot gold is commonly quoted in US dollars per troy ounce. We convert the quoted price to AED per gram using 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce and an indicative USD/AED rate of 3.6725. The 22K, 21K and 18K estimates reflect their nominal gold content, not official shop prices. Dubai Jewellery Group publishes suggested retail jewellery rates separately.

Compare like for like at the gold souk

Ask for the same karat, net gold weight, gold rate, making charge and tax treatment when comparing two shops. Stones, clasps and other components may need separate pricing. Request a written breakdown before paying.

How the estimate is calculated

Gold value = weight in grams × shop rate. Add making charges and other taxable charges, then apply the entered VAT percentage to that subtotal. This assumes the entered shop rate is before VAT. A troy ounce and an ordinary household ounce are not the same unit.

Gold price FAQs

Are these official Dubai gold rates?

No. They are indicative values calculated from international XAU spot data. Check the Dubai Jewellery Group rate board or your jeweller for current suggested retail rates and enter that price in the calculator.

What is the difference between 22K and 24K gold?

24K represents nearly pure gold. 22K contains approximately 22/24 gold by nominal purity; the remaining alloy affects durability and colour.

How much is one tola in grams?

One traditional bullion tola is approximately 11.6638 grams. Verify the actual weighed amount with the seller.

Does the gold rate include making charges or VAT?

Our spot estimates exclude both. Retail quotes and invoices vary, so ask the seller whether each quoted amount includes tax. The calculator expects pre-tax input amounts.

What if the live price cannot be loaded?

You can still use the calculator by entering your shop's AED-per-gram rate. We will not substitute an invented live price.
